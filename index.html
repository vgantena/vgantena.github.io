About Me

A test automation leader with engineering depth and a manager's discipline.

I am a Senior QA Test Automation Lead and IT Manager with over a decade of experience architecting test automation frameworks for enterprise platforms in loyalty, banking, and SaaS. I currently lead a 38+ member cross-functional QA team as Technical and Resource Manager, owning end-to-end deliverables across manual, automation, and performance testing.

My core stack is Karate DSL for API automation, Playwright for UI testing, and Jenkins / GitHub Actions for CI/CD integration. I have shipped 10K+ automated tests across 15+ enterprise clients and 25+ environments, sustaining 99%+ test success rates across every release cycle.

I am especially passionate about applying AI to QA — designing autonomous test agents, self-healing locators, natural-language test generation, and AI-driven coverage governance. Beyond engineering, I drive release estimations, stakeholder sign-offs, appraisals, skill development, and new-tool design and training across the QA organization.
