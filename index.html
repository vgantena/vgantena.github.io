About Me

A snapshot of who I am and what I do.

I am a Senior QA Test Automation Lead and IT Manager with over a decade of experience architecting test automation frameworks for enterprise SaaS, banking, and loyalty platforms. I currently lead a 38+ member cross-functional QA team as Technical and Resource Manager, owning end-to-end deliverables across manual, automation, and performance testing.

My focus is on building scalable test infrastructure using Karate DSL for API automation and Playwright for UI testing, integrated with CI/CD pipelines and modern observability stacks. I am passionate about applying AI to QA — designing autonomous agents, self-healing locators, and natural-language test generation that meaningfully reduce engineering toil.

Beyond engineering, I drive release estimations, stakeholder sign-offs, appraisals, skill development, and new-tool design and training across the organization.
